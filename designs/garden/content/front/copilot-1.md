# Trachelospermum jasminoides — Planting & Installation Q&A

**Context:** Planting a Star Jasmine climber in the front garden, in the soil near the wall by the front door. Trellis purchased: [Forest Garden Hidcote Diamond Lattice Trellis 1800 × 300 mm (Wickes, product 256894)](https://www.wickes.co.uk/Forest-Garden-Hidcote-Diamond-Lattice-Trellis---1800-x-300mm/p/256894) — pressure treated timber, 8 mm battens, 90 mm gap spacing, 28 mm frame.

---

## Q1: Will it self-cling, or does it need tying to a trellis?

**Star Jasmine is a twining climber — it does NOT self-cling.**

It winds stems around a support but will not grip a bare wall the way ivy does. You must:
- Tie main stems in manually when young, using soft garden twine or fabric plant ties in a loose figure-of-8.
- Guide shoots through or around the trellis for the first 1–2 seasons.
- Once established it will weave through the trellis naturally, but young growth needs directing.

---

## Q2: How far from the wall should it be planted?

**Plant 30–45 cm away from the wall.**

- The soil right against a wall sits in a **rain shadow** — significantly drier and often poor in nutrients.
- Roots need room to spread laterally.
- A gap also allows maintenance access behind the plant.

Lean the plant's canes slightly toward the wall at planting so the stems naturally head toward the trellis from day one.

---

## Q3: Trellis setup — height, battens, and full installation method

### Trellis panel count
Each Hidcote panel is 1800 mm tall × 300 mm wide. For one plant, **3–4 panels side by side** (~900–1200 mm wide) gives a good spread to grow into.

### Battens — size and quantity

Vertical timber battens are fixed to the wall first; the trellis panels are then screwed to them. This creates an air gap between trellis and wall — essential for stem circulation and to protect the wall from damp.

| Detail | Specification |
|---|---|
| Batten size | **50 mm × 25 mm** pressure treated timber |
| Batten length | **1800 mm** (matching panel height) |
| Air gap created | 38–50 mm off the wall |
| Number of battens | One at each outer edge + one where panels meet = **4 battens for 3 panels**, **5 battens for 4 panels** |
| Fixings to wall | Masonry drill + rawlplugs + screws (for brick/render wall) |

### Starting height of the trellis

**Fix the bottom of the trellis approximately 15–20 cm above ground level.**

The plant is currently ~80–90 cm tall in its pot. Do not start the trellis 80 cm up the wall — that leaves a long unsupported gap. Starting low means you can tie stems in immediately after planting.

### Step-by-step installation

1. **Mark batten positions** on the wall — vertical lines spaced to match the edges and joints of your trellis panels.
2. **Drill and fix battens** to the wall using appropriate masonry fixings. Use a spirit level.
3. **Screw trellis panels to battens** through the outer perimeter frame of each panel. Bottom of panels ~15–20 cm off the ground.
4. **Prepare the planting hole** 30–40 cm from the wall: dig at least twice the width and depth of the pot; mix in garden compost.
5. **Plant**, angling canes and stems toward the trellis.
6. **Tie in immediately** — soft twine or fabric ties in a loose figure-of-8 so the tie doesn't cut into stems. Spread stems across the width of the trellis rather than letting everything go straight up the centre.
7. **Water thoroughly** and apply a mulch ring (keeping mulch clear of the stem itself).

### Ongoing
Star Jasmine is slow to establish in year one — focus on watering during dry spells. Growth accelerates noticeably in year two once roots are settled.
