# Trachelospermum jasminoides in a Pot — Setup & Care Q&A

**Context:** Same Star Jasmine climber as the front garden, but planted in a pot in the back garden against a wall with trellis support. This Q&A covers how the pot setup differs from the soil planting in the front.

*See also: front garden installation notes in `designs/garden/content/front/copilot-1.md`.*

---

## Q: How does being in a pot change the setup vs soil?

### Distance from wall

In soil, the plant must go **30–45 cm from the wall** (rain shadow, dry soil). In a pot this does not apply — you are watering it yourself. Place the pot **5–10 cm from the wall**, just enough for air circulation behind it. Closer is better so stems reach the trellis easily.

---

### Pot size

This is the most important decision. Star Jasmine will struggle in an undersized pot.

| Spec | Minimum | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Diameter | 40 cm | 50 cm+ |
| Depth | 40 cm | 50 cm+ |
| Material | Any with drainage holes | Heavy (terracotta/stone) — prevents toppling as the plant grows and gets top-heavy |

Always use **pot feet** underneath to allow drainage and airflow from the base.

Fill with good quality peat-free multipurpose compost mixed with ~20% horticultural grit to keep drainage sharp.

---

### Trellis bottom height — key difference from soil planting

The pot raises the plant off the ground. If the pot is ~45 cm tall, the plant's stems are already starting at ~45 cm above the ground. Adjust accordingly:

- **Fix the bottom of the trellis at roughly pot-rim height**, or just 5 cm above the rim
- Do **not** start the trellis at ground level — there will be a dead gap between trellis bottom and where stems emerge from the pot
- Tie stems directly from the pot rim onto the trellis immediately after positioning

---

### Battens, screws, and panel count

Identical to the front garden setup:
- **3–4 Hidcote panels** (1800 × 300 mm) side by side
- **50 mm × 25 mm** pressure treated battens, 1800 mm tall
- **4–5 battens** (one at each outer edge, one at each panel joint)
- Screw/plug options: frame fixings (7.5 × 72 mm) or 6 mm × 70 mm screw + brown wall plug + 7 mm masonry bit
- Trellis-to-batten: 5 mm × 50 mm exterior wood screws (stainless or exterior-coated)

---

### Ongoing care — key differences vs soil

| Topic | Soil (front) | Pot (back) |
|---|---|---|
| Watering | Weekly in dry spells | **Every 2–3 days in summer** — pots dry out fast |
| Feeding | Rarely needed | **Monthly liquid feed** (April–September) — nutrients wash out with watering |
| Frost | Roots fully protected by ground | Roots exposed — wrap pot in horticultural fleece in hard frost (below −5 °C) |
| Re-potting | Never | Every 2–3 years into a slightly larger pot; refresh compost |
| Drying out | Low risk | High risk — if it wilts, water immediately and deeply |
