## 2. Lighter brown

**Image:** `b-lighter-brown.png` · [Open full image](/design-assets/house/ground-floor/reception-room-04/b-lighter-brown.png)

The field is lifted to a lighter warm mushroom/camel brown, with softer taupe-brown floral detail and a lighter border. It remains a brown traditional rug rather than becoming a cream or white one. The prompt asks for a noticeably greater lift than the earlier subtle comparisons, approximately 20–25% as an artistic direction, not a measured colour value.

The purpose is to see whether a softer, lighter ground sits better with the green chairs and oak floor, or whether the deeper rug gives the seating area more desirable weight. Neither direction is selected yet.

Keep the two green chairs, existing cushion pairing, small table, mixed shelf objects/books, still-life painting, piano-wall landscape, lamps, bay study and piano in their current proposed positions.

Wall, ceiling and joinery colours, mouldings, windows, fireplace, floor and black steel glazing remain part of the original house. No painting or new decor is proposed. The comparison is one doorway angle, chosen to keep attention on rug tone.
