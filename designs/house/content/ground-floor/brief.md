This is the living record for the ground floor redesign at 92 Woodstock Road. It captures inputs, constraints, decisions and open questions as the conversation progresses, so nothing lives only in chat history. It is updated as we go; design iterations themselves live in separate versioned files.

## 1. The house

- **Address:** 92 Woodstock Road, Bedford Park, Chiswick, London W4.
- **Type:** Victorian house in Bedford Park.
- **Context note:** Bedford Park is a late-Victorian garden suburb (built from 1875), largely Queen Anne Revival / Arts and Crafts in character, and a conservation area with many listed houses. The design should respect that character. Whether this specific house is listed is an open question (see §7), as it affects what can physically change.
