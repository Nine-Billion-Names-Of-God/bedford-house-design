## 6. Measurements before selecting a rug

Use tape or a paper template in the room to locate the revised rug perimeter. Start with the existing proposed seating footprint and pull its doorway edge back until there is a modest oak strip, rather than buying a much smaller standard size by default.

Check that both armchairs' front feet remain comfortably on the rug, the office chair can roll without crossing a rug edge, the doors operate freely and the rug/table respect the actual hearth clearances. A small doorway margin alone does not establish full door-swing clearance.

No new exact overall rug size is specified here. The earlier approximate 180 × 240 / 200 × 250 cm template suggestions were never measured product sizes; revise them from actual chair positions and the intended gap. The user's feedback is about the fit relationship, not an instruction to order one of those dimensions.

Keep a matte, low-pile surface and a floor-compatible underlay. Compare real samples against the green chairs, oak and wall colour in both daylight and lamp light; the generated colours are not physical swatches.
