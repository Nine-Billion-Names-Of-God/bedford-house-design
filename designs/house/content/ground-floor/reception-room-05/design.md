## 1. The V1 design and its reasoning

The brief gives this room an 1880s Bedford Park library and music-room identity, with three practical uses retained: reading beside the fireplace, playing the piano and working in the bay. Its focal point is the household's panoramic skyline drawing above the mantel, with family photographs and existing personal objects retained within a usable library.

The main textile changes from the earlier brown Persian explorations to an Arts & Crafts rug with large scrolling acanthus leaves and flower heads. The indigo ground, madder red, sage, ochre and cream give it a different character from the hall's geometric terracotta-brown runner. Plain red cushions pick up the rug without adding another pattern. Imari porcelain contributes iron red, cobalt and gold, while the two new lamps use dark antique bronze.

No painting, new joinery or architectural alterations are proposed. The green-and-cream bentwood armchairs, piano and bench, oak desk, two monitors, task chair, dracaena, fireplace, alcoves, chandelier, curtains and glazing remain.

## 7. Measure and confirm before selecting pieces

- Mark the rug with a floor template: verify the 20–25 cm rear-screen gap, both chair front feet, a clear piano-bench zone and the task chair's full rolling area on oak. Do not order from the rendered footprint.
- Check the real pedestal beneath the desk through the desk's full sit-stand movement, without obstructing legs, cables or chair use.
- Measure the piano wall for the proposed map and its clearance above the piano top.
- Check socket positions and cable routes for both lamps.
- Select an actual suitable local map and actual porcelain after the design is reviewed; the rendered map and porcelain are not product recommendations.
