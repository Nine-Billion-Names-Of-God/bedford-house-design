## 1. What V2 asks this room to do

The TV area is the mid-century step between the period front rooms and the contemporary kitchen. Its materials connect those spaces: walnut relates to the kitchen island, charcoal stone and black steel relate to the worktop and glazing, and cognac leather provides a different seating material from the green reception chairs and cream dining chairs.

The household's own collection remains the decoration: the ladder shelf's figures, vessels, plate, red glass and books, the red Vietnamese hat and the large round clock. No new painting or wall art is introduced. The rug is mostly plain oat with sparse geometry, distinct from both the hall runner and reception-room Arts & Crafts rug. Terracotta enters through the lamp glaze and a small rug accent.

V2 retains V1's furniture scheme but explicitly requires original-photo edits, the listed removals, a mounted TV, the hat above the shelf and the dog bed at the kitchen end of the sofa. No optional blanket is included in this first rendering.

## 7. Practical notes from V2

The brief prefers semi-aniline or pigmented leather for the proposed sofa and notes that aniline leather marks and develops a patina. Check actual upholstery samples and manufacturer care information for this household's dog use; the generated leather appearance establishes no durability rating. If leather is not wanted, V2 records deep olive or ink-blue wool in the same shape as alternatives; neither is rendered or selected here.

Measure before choosing pieces:

- Full sofa length **together with the real bed at full size**, including the wall return, island/stool area and clear circulation. Revise through the next brief if both cannot fit; do not reduce the bed in an image to solve it.
- The route between coffee table and sideboard, sideboard depth and door/device access.
- TV height from the chosen sofa's seated eye line, wall mounting provision and cable route to storage.
- Rug footprint with a floor template, retaining exposed oak and access through the screen.
- Suitable non-slip rug underlay compatible with the floor and any underfloor heating.

The second clock on the kitchen wall is reserved for the kitchen stage and is not changed now. All new pieces remain unsourced concepts. V2 prefers genuine vintage pieces for the sideboard and lamp; no particular product, maker or authenticity is claimed by these images.
