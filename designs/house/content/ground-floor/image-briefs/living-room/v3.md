## 1. Design intent: the collector's snug

A **London townhouse snug**: the room where the family actually sits, so it should feel deep, warm and personal, curated rather than decorated. Think of a Notting Hill or Chelsea townhouse sitting room. English antiques are mixed with one or two Asian pieces, a velvet sofa in a confident colour, a faded rug, a good painting with its own light, and a lamp with a coloured shade glowing in the corner. This is the same collector's spirit as the rest of the house, and it suits the user's own collection on the shelf: the seated figure, the red hat and the red glass.

**Colour story (new to the house):**

| Role | Colour | Where |

**Plum** is new to the house, yet it is the natural partner of both the green front room and the rust kitchen. Seen through the glazed screen, the plum sofa and the green reception chairs complement each other.

## 7. Notes to record in `design.md`

- **Velvet and the dog:**
  - Use a performance or tightly woven cotton velvet; it resists claws better than loose weaves.
  - Pale dog hair will show on plum; a lint roller and a washable cover for the dog's favourite end help.
- **The cabinet:**
  - Antique Chinese elm cabinets are widely available in London; check the depth against the walkway.
  - It needs a cable hole drilled at the back and ventilation for the devices.
- **Measure on site:**
  - the sofa length together with the dog bed, both at full size;
  - the walkway between the coffee table and the cabinet;
  - the TV height, from the seated eye line;
  - the rug size, with a floor template;
  - the rug underlay, which must suit underfloor heating.
- **The painting:**
  - It is a placeholder for a real 19th-century-style floral oil. If the user owns a painting they would rather hang here, it takes priority.
  - Where the clock goes is the user's choice; it is not shown.
  - The picture light can be a rechargeable one if there is no wiring.
- **The black ladder shelf:** it stays because it holds the collection. A dark-wood étagère could replace it later if the user wants the room fully wooden; that is not part of this brief.
- **Concepts only:** all new pieces are concepts, not sourced products. Genuine antique or vintage pieces are preferred to reproductions.
