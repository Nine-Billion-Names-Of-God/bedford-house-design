## 1. Design intent: the mid-century hinge

The front of the house is the 1880s (the Aesthetic hall, the library and music room) and the kitchen is today. The TV area takes **the era in between, mid-century (about 1955–75)**, so the eye steps from the period front to the modern kitchen instead of jumping between them:

- **Wood:** the walnut sofa legs and sideboard relate to the walnut island.
- **Stone and steel:** the charcoal stone and black steel of the coffee table relate to the kitchen worktop, the charcoal units and the black glazed screen.
- **Leather:** cognac leather sits between the green velvet at the front and the rust velvet stools at the back.

**The hero is the user's own collection:**
- the ladder shelf of figures, pots, a plate and a red glass piece
- the red Vietnamese hat
- the large round clock

**No new art.** The mid-century pieces are the setting for the collection.

**The terracotta thread** here is in the glaze of the ceramic lamp and the small accents in the rug.

**Earlier user preferences this design keeps:**
- the TV is wall-mounted
- the slick floating cabinet was rejected as too modern
- older pieces with more character are wanted
- the rug must be unlike those elsewhere
- the user's shelf ornaments must not be replaced
- the sofa and TV storage can change
- no painting
- the dog bed stays
