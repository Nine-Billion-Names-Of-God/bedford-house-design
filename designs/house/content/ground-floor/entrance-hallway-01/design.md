## The colour thread

Terracotta enters the hall only through textiles, ceramics and art: the stair runner, the hall runner, the plant pot, the dog lead and the arch print. Nothing is painted. Aged brass is the one metal finish for new items (lamp, tray, rods, hooks), matching the existing brass on the front door.

## The nook: a finding from the drawing

Measured on the architect's plan, the nook to the right of the front door is only about **0.5 m wide × 0.5 m deep**. The stained-glass side light fills most of its back wall. Two consequences:

- **The coat stand and the hamper can't share the nook.** A bentwood stand needs a footprint about 0.4–0.45 m across. This mockup gives the nook to the stand and moves the hamper under the console, which suits a hamper of roughly 45 × 30 cm.
- **Coats on the stand will cover the lower part of the side light.** Keep the stand to two or three everyday coats so the glass still reads.

To confirm on site: the nook's width and depth, the clear depth under the console, and the hamper's size.

## Item notes

- **Bentwood stand:** about 1.85–1.95 m tall, footprint under 45 cm, with an umbrella ring. Dark walnut or black stain to match the console.
- **Lamp:** about 45–55 cm tall, so it sits below the mirror. The drawing shows a socket on this wall; if there isn't one as built, use a cordless rechargeable lamp.
- **Aspidistra:** pot about 20–25 cm diameter in plain terracotta or a rust glaze, with a saucer to protect the console top.
- **Coir mat:** about 90 × 60 cm, plain natural colour with a dark border.
- **Stair runner:** wool, flat or subtle herringbone weave, fitted with underlay, rods at the base of each riser. Professional fitting.
- **Hall runner:** flatweave on a non-slip underlay suitable for underfloor heating; check the clear width beside the stair before buying (60 cm target).
- **Art:** one frame finish throughout; picture hooks into the panels (agreed acceptable).
